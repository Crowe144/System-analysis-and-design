Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The client was DriverPass, a company that wanted to help people prepare for their driving tests through online classes, practice tests, and driving lessons. They needed a system that would allow customers to access these services, schedule appointments, and manage their information online. The system also needed to allow employees to manage customers, appointments, drivers, vehicles, and training packages.

What did you do particularly well?

I think I did particularly well at breaking down the requirements and identifying how the different parts of the system would work together. For instance, when a customer schedules a driving lesson, the system needs to account for the available drivers, vehicles, dates, and times. I also think I did well identifying the different users and what each of them would need access to.

If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I could revise one part of my work, I would probably go back and improve the UML diagrams. While they show how the system should function, I think some of the processes could be broken down further to account for situations that might not go as expected. For instance, what happens when a customer tries to schedule an appointment and there are no available drivers or vehicles? Including more of these situations would make the diagrams more complete.

How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I interpreted the user's needs by reviewing the information provided by DriverPass and identifying what each type of user needed the system to do. For instance, customers needed to schedule appointments and take practice tests, while employees needed to manage appointments, customer information, and training packages. These requirements helped determine the different processes, objects, and relationships included in the system design. Considering the user's needs is important because even if a system works correctly, it doesn't necessarily mean it solves the problem it was designed for.

How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

When designing software, I think it's important to understand what the system needs to accomplish before actually trying to build it. I prefer breaking larger problems into smaller pieces and figuring out how those pieces will work together. In the future, I would continue using UML diagrams to help organize the different processes and relationships within a system. I would also spend more time considering situations where something might go wrong, rather than only focusing on how the system should work under normal circumstances.
